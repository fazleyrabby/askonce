# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Laravel 13, Blade, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Horizon, FrankenPHP and Docker Compose, as specified in spec.md.

Use native `fetch` for browser HTTP requests. Do not add Axios.

## Users
Freelancers and small agencies collecting material from clients. Clients use a no-login request link on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose
Ask once. Stop chasing. Collect files, text, links and confirmations in one request, with automatic reminders for missing items.

## Operating Context
Businesses create requests, share secure links and monitor completion. Clients save each item independently and can resume later. Completion stops reminders.

## Capabilities and Constraints
V1 is a free beta. No credentials, billing, templates, integrations, team workspaces or project management. Organization ownership applies to every business-owned record. Uploads are private. Full product truth and roadmap are in spec.md.

## Brand Commitments
AskOnce. Plain, concise language. User delegated visual judgment and selected direct implementation in code.

## Evidence on Hand
spec.md supplies product requirements. No logos, testimonials or customer proof supplied.

## Product Principles
- Keep the client experience simple and account-free.
- Save work as it happens.
- Remind only while material is missing.
- Protect tenant data and uploaded files.
